You’ve Discovered Your Direction. Now Let’s Navigate Your Journey.

Meet NAVI: Your Purpose Transition Pathways

NOVA helps you discover your natural strengths and possible direction. NAVI introduces the next step: exploring how to put that direction into practice through education, training, experience, and career planning. You do not necessarily need to leave your current job to begin.

Make Your Purpose Transition Plan

At Step 3, explore four routes. All four reconnect at Step 4.

Review the purpose insights and strengths NOVA revealed. Choose a field or contribution aligned with your purpose.

Parallel Career Transition

Progressive Transition

↓
6Review Your Progress

At 30 and 90 days, assess your learning, experience and next step.

For regulated professions, verify requirements with the appropriate licensing authority before changing jobs or paying for training.

NAVI Learning & Training Resources

Explore independent learning resources that may help you prepare for your chosen direction. Start with introductory courses, then compare certificates, apprenticeships, technical training, and degree programs according to your goals.

  • Coursera — University courses and professional certificates
  • edX — Academic and technical subjects
  • Khan Academy — Free foundational learning
  • LinkedIn Learning — Business, technology, and workplace skills
  • Udemy — Practical software, technical, and creative skills
  • Apprenticeship.gov — Registered apprenticeship opportunities
  • CareerOneStop — Explore careers and locate training

These are independent third-party resources, not NOVA- or NAVI-sponsored programs or endorsements. Course availability, cost, credential recognition, and entry requirements vary. Confirm details directly with each provider and check licensing requirements for regulated occupations.
