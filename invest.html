NOVA Purpose Transition Pathways

Discovering your purpose is the beginning. Walking in it does not always require leaving your current job. Choose a transition pathway that fits your skills, financial responsibilities, and readiness.

Direct Transition

Move into a new field when your transferable skills and qualifications already meet its entry requirements.

Preparation-First Transition

Complete needed schooling, technical training, certification, or licensing before changing fields.

Parallel Career Transition

Keep your current career while building and practicing a second career aligned with your purpose.

Progressive Transition

Make a series of smaller changes through courses, projects, experience, and intermediate roles.

Make your transition plan

  1. Identify your destination: Choose one field or contribution you want to explore.
  2. Check requirements: Compare your existing experience with the education, credentials, and licensing the field requires.
  3. Select your pathway: Decide whether direct, preparation-first, parallel, or progressive change fits your situation.
  4. Protect your stability: Estimate training costs, time commitments, and how you will maintain income during any transition.
  5. Start small: Within seven days, research a program, talk to someone in the field, or try a relevant project.
  6. Review your progress: At 30 and 90 days, reassess your learning, experience, and next step.

For regulated professions, verify requirements with the appropriate licensing authority before changing jobs or paying for training.
