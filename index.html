What NOVA looks for

These are the clues NOVA considers.

You don’t have to know the perfect answer. Start with the things you say in everyday life. Skip any question that doesn’t fit.

1

What you enjoy

“I enjoy cooking.” “I love reading.” “I could spend all day creating.”

2

What comes naturally

Subjects and activities that felt comfortable, interesting, or easier for you.

3

What others rely on you for

“People come to me to fix things, solve problems, give advice, or figure out what to do.”

4

Your traits and patterns

The strengths and ways of working you recognize in yourself across those experiences.

↓
Then NOVA compares those clues with occupational information.

The Occupational Outlook Handbook describes hundreds of occupations, including their work, preparation, pay, and outlook. NOVA uses those profiles to help surface purpose roles and show why they may fit your answers.

NOVA’s results are recommendations to help you explore. They are based on the answers you choose and the occupation information available; they don’t decide your life for you.
